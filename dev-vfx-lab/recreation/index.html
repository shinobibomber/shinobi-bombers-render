What differs from the poster

Detailed dragon, crescent, eclipse and vortex artwork is recreated, not an exact extraction. Motion uses consistent master shapes, controlled deformation and interpolation. The supplied poster contains 7/8/9/11 separable drawings; this preview supplies 8/8/10/12 frames.

Dragon scales, chain placement, particle paths and some energy contours differ. Original reusable crescent, lightning, sigil and impact elements are included where clean transparency was available. These are cosmetic previews; poster descriptions do not add damage, pulls, stuns or rewards.

Front/back selects the original hero’s view. The VFX remain a single camera-facing effect plane; left mirrors that plane. The original character, aura and current rarity metadata are not edited.

Transparent sprite exports

Each effect has ground, foreground and combined RGBA PNG sheets, four columns, 512 × 512 cells, no spacing, anchor (256, 420), and JSON frame rectangles and durations.

Video samples

Each sample shows slowed formation on a dark background followed by native timing on the Adventure map.
