**⚔️ SHINOBI BOMBERS — PATCH NOTES (Nov 3, 2026)**

**✨ New**
• **Hero Auras** — every rarity now has its own animated aura: a ground ring, a glowing wreath and rising sparks. Elite and Legend shine brightest.
• **Global Chat** — tap the chat bar on Home to chat live. The System tab announces every Legend summoned or fused! ⭐
• **Summon History** — see every hero you've summoned, batch by batch.
• **New header** — live Players Online count, a ⚙️ settings menu and a one-tap Connect / Disconnect wallet button.

**🔄 Changes**
• The original animated hero sprites are back in menus and in battle.
• Summon reveals show your actual hero with its aura. Elite and Legend get a bright glow, and single summons are sharp.
• **Boss Hunt** — your 5 strongest rested heroes fight. Cleaner screen, no more blurry text.
• **Fusion** — every Reserve hero is listed. Elites below Lv 10 show a 🔒 (Elite → Legend needs three Lv 10 Elites).
• **Hero limit: 500** — fuse heroes to free slots before summoning more.
• **Adventure** — a bigger battlefield, the stage list is gone, and defeated enemies show the Ryo they pay for their tier.

**🛠️ Fixes**
• Heroes and enemies no longer stand on walls or obstacles; each map's solid tiles now match its board.
• Enemies spawn spread out instead of in clumps.
• Deposits refresh your balance automatically, so you can make another transaction right away.
• Fixed overlapping Strike Team slots, a black strip under the header and black bars around the map.

Thanks for playing — see you on the battlefield! 🥷
