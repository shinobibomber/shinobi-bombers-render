Approved service revenue split

Eligible net SR revenue from approved game services only. Treasury → reward pool replenishment is an internal transfer and is never split again. No automatic burn.

Optional Expedition early-return payments use their own split: 50% Active Reward Pool, 30% Treasury, 10% Community Reward Vault, 10% Content Creators Reward Pool. Nothing is burned. The 10% wallet was previously called the Economic Stability Reserve; its address and share are unchanged.

      Burn Reserve governance

      Squads multisig approval protects the Burn Reserve: a burn or transfer needs the approval threshold. It is not a timelock, and it does not by itself restrict what an approved proposal may do.

      SR token mint

      Total supply is read from the mint. Pump.fun does not assign these allocations automatically, and it does not label the Burn Reserve as burned.

      Rules and honest limits

        • Withdrawals of claimed SR return 5% to the Treasury. That deduction has its own ledger and is never counted as service revenue.
        • The Treasury, Active Reward Pool, Community Reward Vault and Content Creators Reward Pool are currently single-key wallets: they carry no on-chain spending restriction. The RYO Burn Reserve is a Squads multisig vault.
        • Moving RYO between these wallets (for example the Treasury move on 12 November 2026) is an internal transfer: it is not revenue and it is not a burn.
        • Balances are finalized observations, refreshed at most once a minute. If the chain cannot be read, values show as unavailable — never as zero.
        • SR revenue routing, Chest claims and withdrawals run under the approved vault configuration, funded reward pools and the daily limits described in the whitepaper.
