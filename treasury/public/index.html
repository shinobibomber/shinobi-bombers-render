Approved service revenue split

Eligible net SR revenue from approved game services only. Treasury → reward pool replenishment is an internal transfer and is never split again. No automatic burn.

Reward claim funding

The four Chapter 04 wallets are owner-controlled and external to the game. Players are paid Chest rewards only from approved top-ups into the game's claim wallet — so these two figures are deliberately kept apart.

SR token mint

Rules and honest limits

    • Withdrawals of claimed SR return 5% to the Locked Treasury. That deduction has its own ledger and is never counted as service revenue.
    • The Active Reward Pool, Community Reward Vault and Economic Stability Reserve are currently single-key wallets: they carry no on-chain spending restriction.
    • Balances are finalized observations, refreshed at most once a minute. If the chain cannot be read, values show as unavailable — never as zero.
    • SR revenue routing, Chest claims and withdrawals open only after the official launch and the owner's explicit activation.
