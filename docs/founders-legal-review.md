# Founder's NFT Boxes: items for legal and commercial review

This checklist is for the owner and their legal adviser. It is **not legal advice**. Paid Mainnet sales must not open until each item is resolved and approved.

## Must be reviewed before any paid Mainnet sale

1. **Randomized reward in a paid package.** Both tiers include Premium Hero Summons (70% Elite / 30% Legend) and 10 Limited Hero Summon Passes (Chunin 62.5% / Jonin 25% / Elite 10% / Legend 2.5%). These are chance-based rewards attached to a purchase. Check them against consumer-protection, loot-box and gambling rules in every market you sell to, including the Philippines. The odds are published on the page and enforced by the server.
2. **Philippine crypto-asset rules.** Check whether selling NFTs for SOL needs registration, a licence or disclosures with the SEC or BSP (for example virtual-asset service-provider rules), and whether the Founder NFT could be treated as a security. The copy avoids investment language, but that doesn't settle the legal question.
3. **Sale disclosures and terms.**
   - The exact benefits.
   - Benefits are bound to the original buyer and do not transfer with the NFT.
   - One per wallet is not one per person.
   - Delayed-launch scenarios.
   - What happens if a benefit can't be delivered.
   - Refund and dispute policy (on-chain payments can't be reversed automatically).
   - The Weekly Mission Pass is a **future obligation** ("arrives with the Missions update").
4. **Benefit transfer policy.** Benefits are bound to the original buyer; the NFT becomes a collectible after transfer. Make sure secondary buyers are told this clearly, including in the NFT metadata (already stated there) and in the marketplace description.
5. **Artwork and IP.** Confirm commercial rights to all poster and NFT artwork and to the hero and character designs. Some poster characters resemble existing franchise styles, so get an IP clearance check.
6. **Tax and record-keeping.** Revenue recognition for SOL received, record-keeping for each purchase (the database stores the signature, price, time and wallet), and any VAT or sales-tax obligations.
7. **Privacy.** Wallet addresses are personal data under some laws. The privacy notice should cover purchase records and the Command Center views.

## Product facts the terms must match (as implemented)

- **Prices and supply:** Mythic 1 SOL, Legacy 0.5 SOL, 30 each, 60 in total, one per wallet across both tiers. Payment is in SOL on Solana.
- **12-hour Chest cooldown:**
  - Applies to verified Founders (both tiers) while Founder rewards are enabled.
  - Regular players stay at 24 hours.
  - Only the claim frequency changes; the reward pool, per-claim maximum and global daily budget are unchanged.
  - A Founder can therefore claim up to twice in 24 hours (owner-approved).
- **Sensei:**
  - Mythic includes free permanent Sensei.
  - Legacy gets a 7-day trial that starts when the buyer activates it. During that trial, permanent Sensei costs 0.2 SOL instead of 0.4 SOL.
  - On the live server, Sensei is no longer sold for Ryo.
- **Game-service discount:** 10% (Mythic) or 5% (Legacy) on Summon, Fusion, Dojo, Sensei and Training. It doesn't stack with the old Mythic Box 10%.
- **Premium Summon schedule:** counts completed Monday–Sunday (UTC) weeks with Adventure play after activation. Missed weeks are not made up.
- **Early access:** eligibility only. It does not open the game until a separately approved stage.
