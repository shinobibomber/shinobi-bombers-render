Chapter 01

Executive Summary

Shinobi Bombers is an anime-inspired blockchain game built around collectible Shinobi NFT heroes, automated Adventure gameplay, character progression, and a player-driven economy.

The project uses a dual-treasury structure separating gameplay token management from real-world operating expenses.

Chapter 02

The Game Ecosystem

⛩ Summoning Shrine

Summon collectible heroes at a base price of 25,000 SR per summon (batches of 5, 10 or 15 are available). Shrine odds: Genin 60%, Chunin 25%, Jonin 10%, Elite 4%, Legend 1%. Legend pity: a Legend is guaranteed on the 150th paid summon in a row without one. Mythic heroes are never drawn at the Shrine; they come from special collections such as the Founder's NFT Boxes.

⚔ Adventure Mode

Deploy up to 10 battle-ready heroes, clear stages, defeat enemies, and collect chest rewards that are added to your Chest as each defeat happens. Each attack spends 1 Chakra; a deployed hero that runs out rests until 10% of its Chakra is back, and a better Dojo shortens the rest. Heroes in Reserve do not recover Chakra. Enemy rewards run from 50% to 100% of the base rate, in 10% steps, as the funded reward pool grows by 100,000 SR per step. A Sensei keeps your team fighting while you are offline, for up to 12 hours.

✦ Fusion

Combine three heroes of the same rarity to attempt the next rarity: Genin→Chunin 90%, Chunin→Jonin 80%, Jonin→Elite 70%, Elite→Legend 60% (Elite→Legend needs three level-5 Elite heroes). Fusion Shards (2 Upgrade Shards craft 1 Fusion Shard) add +5% success each per attempt, up to a guaranteed 100%. Fusion service fees use SR. Shards are account-bound game items.

🌸 Dojo Services

Dojo upgrades speed up Chakra recovery and raise your Chest claim allowance by 100,000 SR per tier (up to +600,000 at Legendary Dojo). Upgrade prices: Apprentice 3,000,000 · Shinobi 7,200,000 · Veteran 14,700,000 · Master 25,200,000 · Grandmaster 38,700,000 · Legendary 55,200,000 SR. Sensei is purchased with SOL (permanent Sensei: 0.4 SOL). The Dojo has no separate hero-training service.

🧬 Training & Upgrade Shards

Heroes level up to level 5 through Training, paid in SR plus Upgrade Shards. Spare heroes can be salvaged for Upgrade Shards (Genin 1, Chunin 2, Jonin 3, Elite 4, Legend 5, Mythic 7). Higher rarities carry more permanent perks, from 1 for Genin to all 7 for Mythic. Shards are account-bound and cannot be traded or withdrawn.

🏪 Hero Marketplace

Buy Heroes, Sell Heroes, and Make Offer. The initial Marketplace is for supported hero NFTs, not general item trading. Account-bound heroes, such as rescued prisoners and referral rewards, cannot be listed. The Marketplace opens in a later roadmap phase.

👹 Boss Hunt

Face challenging bosses with an automatic 60-second team attack and participate in Daily Rewards and Weekly Rewards under published eligibility and reward-pool rules. Each 10,000 SR entry splits 80% to the prize pool, 10% to the Locked Treasury and 10% burned. Boss Hunt opens in a later roadmap phase.

🏕️ Expedition Mode

Live. After 10 hours of actual combat a hero leaves on a free Expedition to rest, then returns on its own. See Expedition Mode below.

👑 Founder's NFT Boxes

Limited Mythic Founder (33) and Legacy Founder (30) boxes with in-game benefits for the buying wallet. See Chapter 11.

🎁 Hero Passes & Starter Activation

Not active yet. Standard Hero Passes (account-bound promotional heroes) and Starter Activation (10 free starter Genin and a 50% enemy reward rate until 15 paid summons, for new players) switch on only when announced.

Expedition Mode

Live since November 11, 2026

Expedition Mode encourages hero rotation. Every hero builds Combat Duty from actual fighting only: each attack the server accepts adds that hero's attack cooldown. Resting, Chakra recovery, the bench, Reserve, idle time and time on an Expedition never count, and a repeated or duplicate report cannot add time twice.

At 10 hours of Combat Duty a hero automatically leaves on a free Expedition. While away it cannot fight, be deployed or enter a Boss Hunt, and Sensei swaps in another hero. Its Chakra keeps recovering on the bench (it is never refilled instantly), and its rank, stats, perks, ownership and NFT data do not change. When the Expedition ends, Combat Duty restarts at zero and the hero returns on its own, including while you are offline. The Expedition Hall on the main menu shows each hero's Combat Duty, who is away and who is back.

Locked Treasury Rules

The Locked Treasury is a Squads v4 multisig vault requiring 2 of 3 signer approvals, with no single-key configuration authority. Service checkout re-verifies it at finalized commitment against the published registry and refuses payment if its signers, threshold or authority change, or if a game-controlled key is a signer. The Treasury is not time-locked so approved reward replenishment stays available. Public addresses and live verification are shown on the Treasury transparency page.

A wallet label is not proof of a lock. The current adapter verifies multisig restrictions, not a timelock or a prohibition on all possible signer actions. The 30% Active Reward allocation is sent to the Active Reward Pool wallet. Adventure chest claims are paid only from verified, independently approved top-ups into the game claim custody, which are internal transfers and are never split again. Approved top-ups can come from the Active Reward Pool, the Locked Treasury (through its Squads vote) or the Content Creators Reward Pool; each needs a finalized on-chain transfer and approval by a second authorized reviewer. Community Rewards and the Content Creators Reward Pool use separate destinations. Boss Hunt, PvP, Sensei SOL and marketplace proceeds are not automatically funded by this pool. Activation requires approved vault configuration.

Important Accounting Distinction The 5% SR deduction collected from player withdrawals goes directly to the Locked Treasury. It is not split again under the 50/30/10/10 service-revenue allocation.

Chapter 05

24-Hour Chest Claim System

Withdrawal of Claimed SR

Eligible Adventure chest rewards may be claimed once every 24 hours (every 12 hours for Founders). This restriction applies to chest claiming, not to withdrawals of successfully claimed balances.

  1. Players participate in Adventure using eligible heroes.
  2. Chest rewards accumulate according to verified game rules.
  3. Players claim eligible chest rewards when the 24-hour cycle allows.
  4. Successfully claimed SR becomes part of the available withdrawal balance.
  5. The next chest claim is available after the verified cooldown expires.

Claim cooldowns use authoritative server timestamps. Failed claims do not consume the cooldown, and duplicate claim processing is prevented.

Claim Limits

  • Minimum claim: 100,000 SR. A claim that cannot reach it is refused rather than paid partly, and the claim timer is not used. Rewards keep adding up safely in the Chest.
  • Maximum per claim: a base allowance plus a Dojo bonus of +100,000 SR per Dojo tier, up to +600,000 at Legendary Dojo. The bonus is added on top of the normal per-claim maximum (for example, a normal limit of 200,000 SR becomes up to 800,000 SR at Legendary Dojo).
  • Claims and Chest purchases share an approved daily reward budget and need a funded Active Reward Pool and custody backing.
  • Chest Overflow: while your unclaimed Chest holds 500,000 SR or more, new enemy rewards are reduced: 500,000+ −10%, 750,000+ −20%, 1,000,000+ −30%. SR already in the Chest is never reduced, and claiming or spending lowers the penalty immediately.

Spending unclaimed rewards. Verified, unclaimed chest SR can also pay for approved game services (Summon, Fusion, Dojo upgrades, Training) at once, without waiting for the claim cooldown and without the withdrawal deduction. A single purchase can use as much of your Chest as it costs. The spend is checked against the funded reward pool, custody backing and the approved daily reward budget, is never counted as a claim, and its revenue follows the 50/30/10/10 allocation. Sensei (SOL), Boss Hunt entry and the Marketplace are excluded.

Unclaimed rewards stay in your Chest across claim cycles; only new rewards are reduced by Chest Overflow.

Withdrawal Rules

  • 5% of the gross SR withdrawal is deducted.
  • 95% of the gross SR withdrawal is paid to the player.
  • The deducted 5% is credited to the Locked SR Treasury.
  • The minimum withdrawal is 100,000 SR, and there is no waiting period between withdrawals.
  • No separate SOL-denominated platform withdrawal service fee is charged.
  • Normal Solana network or token-account creation costs remain distinct.
  • Fees and final payout must be shown before player confirmation.

Security Requirements

The withdrawal system must verify ownership and balances, prevent duplicate payouts, maintain adequate backing, and reconcile fee and player-payout movements safely.

Deposits

  • Deposits are free: 100% of the SR you deposit is credited to your game balance, up to 10,000,000 SR per deposit.
  • Every deposit and withdrawal ends with an on-screen result: successful, failed with the reason, or processing until it is confirmed on-chain.
  • A deposit that never reached Solana (for example after a dropped connection) is cleared automatically once the network can no longer accept it, usually within about 3 minutes. No tokens leave your wallet unless the deposit reaches Solana, and a deposit that lands late is still credited exactly once.

Chapter 10

Reward Recycling & Sustainability

The project aims to recycle eligible SR revenue and withdrawal deductions into controlled reward funding.

  1. Players participate in eligible gameplay activities.
  2. Verified chest rewards are funded from the Active Reward Pool.
  3. Players claim eligible rewards on the 24-hour cycle.
  4. Claimed SR becomes available for withdrawal.
  5. Successful withdrawal returns 5% SR to the Locked Treasury.
  6. Approved Treasury releases may replenish reward reserves.

Three Potential Treasury Funding Sources

50%
Of full approved SR game-service payments
5%
Of successful gross SR withdrawals
Optional
SR bought with approved surplus SOL

These streams are accounted for separately. Withdrawal deductions must not be counted again as newly collected service revenue. Optional Expedition early returns add a further stream: 30% of each payment goes to the Locked Treasury and 50% to the Active Reward Pool, which reaches Chest rewards only through approved top-ups.

Reward flow is also limited by the approved daily reward budget, the 100,000 SR minimum claim, per-claim maximums, and Chest Overflow.

Reward budgets must be constrained by actual available funds, verified player obligations, and realistic expected game revenue.

Sustainability Is Not Guaranteed Treasury recycling does not create unlimited SR. Players can sell SR after it reaches their wallets. No reward model or treasury allocation can guarantee token price stability.

Chapter 15

Risks & Limitations

Token Price Risk

SR prices may fluctuate significantly. Buybacks do not guarantee a price floor or market capitalization.

Reward Funding Risk

A locked reserve and withdrawal deductions do not guarantee unlimited reward funding.

NFT Market Risk

NFTs may decline in value or have limited resale liquidity. Ownership does not guarantee investment returns.

Blockchain & Security Risk

Smart contracts, wallets, trading venues, custody services, and infrastructure can experience technical failures.

Withdrawal Risk

Network disruption, insufficient backing, or security incidents may delay withdrawal settlement.

Legal & Development Risk

Financial features may require legal review, and development milestones may change after testing.

Payment Timing Risk

Expedition early-return payments are final on-chain. A payment confirmed after the Expedition has already ended brings no benefit and cannot be reversed. Keep the game open until the payment is confirmed.

Game Balance Risk

Expedition limits and prices, Chest Overflow, claim limits and reward rates may be adjusted after launch based on how the economy behaves.
