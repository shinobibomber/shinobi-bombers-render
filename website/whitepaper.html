Chapter 01

Executive Summary

Shinobi Bombers is an anime-inspired blockchain game built around collectible Shinobi NFT heroes, automated Adventure gameplay, character progression, and a player-driven economy.

The project proposes a dual-treasury structure separating gameplay token management from real-world operating expenses.

$SR

Game Economy

Supports eligible gameplay services, Adventure chest rewards, community incentives, and long-term reward reserves.

SOL

Development Economy

Supports development, hosting, security, infrastructure, marketing, and approved future economic activities.

Our Economic Principles Transparency · Responsible reward distribution · Operational sustainability · Continuous game development.

Locked Treasury Rules

Service checkout verifies an external SPL multisig Treasury requiring at least two signers and excluding the game custody signer. Treasury or Reserve replenishment is recognized only after a successful finalized transfer and independent administrative approval.

A wallet label is not proof of a lock. The current adapter verifies multisig restrictions, not a timelock or a prohibition on all possible signer actions. The Active Reward allocation remains in game custody with a separate Adventure funding cap; Community and Reserve use separate destinations. Boss Hunt, PvP, Sensei SOL and marketplace proceeds are not automatically funded by this pool. Activation requires approved vault configuration.

Important Accounting Distinction The 5% SR deduction collected from player withdrawals goes directly to the Locked Treasury. It is not split again under the 50/30/10/10 service-revenue allocation.
Chapter 05

24-Hour Chest Claim System

24H
Chest Reward Claim Interval
Anytime
Withdrawal of Claimed SR

Eligible Adventure chest rewards may be claimed once every 24 hours. This restriction applies to chest claiming, not to withdrawals of successfully claimed balances.

  1. Players participate in Adventure using eligible heroes.
  2. Chest rewards accumulate according to verified game rules.
  3. Players claim eligible chest rewards when the 24-hour cycle allows.
  4. Successfully claimed SR becomes part of the available withdrawal balance.
  5. The next chest claim is available after the verified cooldown expires.

Claim cooldowns must use authoritative timestamps. Failed claims must not incorrectly consume the cooldown, and duplicate claim processing must be prevented.

The final policy must clarify how unclaimed rewards are treated across multiple claim cycles.

Withdrawal Rules

  • 5% of the gross SR withdrawal is deducted.
  • 95% of the gross SR withdrawal is paid to the player.
  • The deducted 5% is credited to the Locked SR Treasury.
  • No separate SOL-denominated platform withdrawal service fee is charged.
  • Normal Solana network or token-account creation costs remain distinct.
  • Fees and final payout must be shown before player confirmation.

Security Requirements

The withdrawal system must verify ownership and balances, prevent duplicate payouts, maintain adequate backing, and reconcile fee and player-payout movements safely.

Liquidity Reserve

Reserve assets may be allocated toward approved liquidity requirements, subject to platform rules, asset availability, treasury governance, and disclosure. These funds are separate from assets already committed to an exchange liquidity pool.

Optional SR Buybacks

Transition Requirement The proposed target is at least 3–6 months of essential operating expenses in reserve, alongside necessary security funding, before discretionary buybacks begin.
Chapter 10

Reward Recycling & Sustainability

The project aims to recycle eligible SR revenue and withdrawal deductions into controlled reward funding.

  1. Players participate in eligible gameplay activities.
  2. Verified chest rewards are funded from the Active Reward Pool.
  3. Players claim eligible rewards on the 24-hour cycle.
  4. Claimed SR becomes available for withdrawal.
  5. Successful withdrawal returns 5% SR to the Locked Treasury.
  6. Approved Treasury releases may replenish reward reserves.

Three Potential Treasury Funding Sources

50%
Of full approved SR game-service payments
5%
Of successful gross SR withdrawals
Optional
SR bought with approved surplus SOL

These streams are accounted for separately. Withdrawal deductions must not be counted again as newly collected service revenue.

Reward budgets must be constrained by actual available funds, verified player obligations, and realistic expected game revenue.

Sustainability Is Not Guaranteed Treasury recycling does not create unlimited SR. Players can sell SR after it reaches their wallets. No reward model or treasury allocation can guarantee token price stability.

Creator Rewards

  • 50,000 CP for each verified referral that mints 15 paid heroes and clears Stage 5.
  • 5% of qualified referrals' eligible settled Adventure SR rewards, credited as CP.
  • 5% of qualified referrals' eligible settled SR-denominated game-service spending, credited as CP.
  • Default proposed commission window: 90 days after referral qualification.
  • The initial 15 qualifying paid hero mints do not also generate purchase commission.

Creator Points Restrictions

CP is a non-transferable, non-withdrawable internal rewards balance. It cannot be exchanged directly for SR, SOL, USDC, or cash. It can only be used in the approved Creator Shop.

Creator Shop redemptions are subject to published inventory, supply, and eligibility limits. The 50,000 CP milestone guarantees the awarded CP, not unlimited NFT inventory.

SOL-Paid Sensei and Creator Commissions Sensei purchases are excluded from the SR-based 5% purchase commission unless the project adopts a separately disclosed SOL-to-CP conversion policy.

Treasury Transparency

Shinobi Bombers proposes public reporting on eligible game revenue, player reward funding, treasury movements, and operating allocations.

SR Reporting

  • Eligible game-service revenue.
  • Revenue allocation by vault.
  • Chest rewards claimed.
  • SR withdrawals and 5% deductions.
  • Treasury transfers and vault balances.
  • Outstanding player reward liabilities.

SOL Reporting

  • Limited NFT Box revenue.
  • Sensei purchase revenue.
  • Eligible creator-fee receipts.
  • Development and hosting expenditure.
  • Emergency and security reserves.
  • Liquidity funding and optional buybacks.

Governance & Verification

Where feasible, reports should reference on-chain transactions and reconcile public holdings with internal account balances. Lock restrictions, administrators, upgrade authority, and emergency permissions must be disclosed.

The proposed reporting cadence is monthly, with more frequent dashboard updates when reliable data is available.

Chapter 15

Risks & Limitations

Token Price Risk

SR prices may fluctuate significantly. Buybacks do not guarantee a price floor or market capitalization.

Reward Funding Risk

A locked reserve and withdrawal deductions do not guarantee unlimited reward funding.

NFT Market Risk

NFTs may decline in value or have limited resale liquidity. Ownership does not guarantee investment returns.

Blockchain & Security Risk

Smart contracts, wallets, trading venues, custody services, and infrastructure can experience technical failures.

Withdrawal Risk

Network disruption, insufficient backing, or security incidents may delay withdrawal settlement.

Legal & Development Risk

Financial features may require legal review, and development milestones may change after testing.
